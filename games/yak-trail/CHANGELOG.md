# Yak Trail — changelog

One short entry per change the user can play, newest first: what changed, the decision numbers behind
it, and how to see it. This is the only place a change is summarized — other files link here.

## 2026-11-02 — music, sounds, F8 knobs, F9 moments and a teaser
- Music: a gentle folk trail theme on the title and the climb, a short fanfare at the summit; a
  little sound for every hop, landing, munch, cairn and fall. The sound starts with the first key;
  paused, the music drops to a murmur. Hear it: play the game, or open Sound in the studio.
- F8 while playing from the studio: slide **Trot speed** and **Hop height**, flip **Trail music**
  between the folk band and quiet bells; Keep the one you like. The defaults play exactly as before.
- F9 now attaches a save of the climb, so an AI can load the exact moment.
- A 12 s teaser clip (9:16): `node cli/yak.mjs clip yak-trail teaser`, or the studio's Clips view.

## 2026-11-02 — the first playable climb
- The whole demo: title, one trail from meadow through rocks to snow, hops, tufts to munch, cairns as
  checkpoints, birds, the summit and its panel, pause with Help and a timer switch, the best time kept
  (#1–#6). Play it from the studio (Games → Yak Trail → Play).
- The text and the boxes are art: the font and the UI widgets show in the Art view and can be repainted.
